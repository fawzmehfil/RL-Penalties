# Third-Party Assets

Stage 9 uses no texture, character, animation, icon, or stadium asset pack. All
visual materials and geometry are project-authored Unity primitives. The only
third-party content is the small audio library below.

Download/import date: 2026-08-07.

All files are committed and bundled in the local demo. OGG previews were used
without waveform editing; source and imported SHA-256 are therefore identical.
